WHO WE ARE

Built on hard work.
Driven by trust.

Love's Auto Garage Ltd. began as a small workshop and has grown into an automotive service centre in Juba, South Sudan. We believe every vehicle deserves expert care and attention.

Our mission is to deliver consistent, high-quality vehicle maintenance and repair through skilled workmanship, modern technology and honest customer care.

Our vision

To become South Sudan's most trusted professional automotive service provider, setting a benchmark for quality, safety and customer satisfaction.
